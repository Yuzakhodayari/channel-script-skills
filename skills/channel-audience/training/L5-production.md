# L5: Production

For each brief, write the first 90 seconds and the final 60 seconds. Annotate every device inline as [A0xx], then score with the SKILL.md rubric (/22) and compute the re-hook gap and "you" density by hand.

1. **Gen mode.** "Your rent is being set by an algorithm." Target "you" ≈28/1k in the open, re-hook ≈15 s.
2. **Adam mode.** "The email that almost ended a company." Target "you" ≈13/1k, re-hook ≈30 s, with one document-based credibility line.
3. **Mechanism mode.** "The one valve that keeps a city's lights on." Fear in the open, agency in the close.
4. **Calibration drill.** Take your Brief 1 open and write a version that is *too* hyped (over the outrage and fear norms). Then fix it and name each norm you restored.
5. **Comment-bait drill.** Write 3 opinion-question comment prompts for Brief 2 and pick the one that ties to the thesis (criterion "Opinion poll question").
