## Drill 3 — Opener library
Tag each line with its card, then write your own on a new topic.

- “if you were a Chinese household with some savings, your options were” → `A01` Viewer inside the example
- “if you put yourself there, this was a major period of change.” → `A02` Put-yourself-there prompt
- “When you hear 2008, you probably picture something like this.” → `A03` Name the viewer's assumption
- “if I had to bet, most of you watching have probably only seen like one real crash.” → `A04` Bet on the viewer's experience
- “before you get on your high horse, soon enough there's a good chance that also becomes you” → `A05` Implicate the viewer (gently)
- “you feel forgotten because nobody seems to be acknowledging what you're experiencing.” → `A06` Name the viewer's feeling
- “I want you to be honest with yourself about what you think of it” → `A07` Ask the viewer to be honest with themselves
- “And look, I'm no saint because I did one a few months ago.” → `A08` Disclose your own stake
- “I've chosen the narrative here. And those choices matter.” → `A09` Check-my-work invitation
- “Look, I don't blame people for not wanting them” → `A10` 'I'm not one of those people' disclaimer
- “in an old bookstore in Portland” → `A11` Source transparency
- “I had to look up what that is.” → `A12` Admit confusion
- “I'm not cherry-picking edge cases. This is Minneapolis.” → `A13` Not-cherry-picking line
- “to be fair to the dude, he's just doing his job.” → `A14` Fair-to-the-dude line
- “Feel free to skip to the next chapter if you'd rather not see those.” → `A15` Content warning with skip
- “we'll get to what that was in a second.” → `A16` Open loop with delayed reveal
- “But hold on, there's more.” → `A17` 'But hold on, there's more' stacker
- “bear with me, it will all make sense in a second.” → `A18` Bear-with-me promise
