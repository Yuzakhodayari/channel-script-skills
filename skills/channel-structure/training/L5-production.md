# L5: Production

For each brief, produce:
1. the mode and its runtime/word target
2. the full beat outline with % and timestamps
3. the promise line
4. the sponsor bridge and re-entry (or "none, Adam mode")
5. chapter titles
6. the conclusion pivot question

Score with the 10-item SKILL.md rubric and check every number against `criteria.md` tests. Compare with `sample.md` for format only. Don't reuse its content.

1. **Brief A (Gen mode).** "Why every concert ticket costs $400 now." Facts: [use real sourced facts only].
2. **Brief B (Adam mode).** "The 72 hours a lab lost control of its AI model." Hypothetical/fictional framing must be labeled as such.
3. **Brief C (Mechanism mode).** "How one canal can stop the world's shipping."
4. **Brief D (Others).** A 15-minute explainer: "Why your city's tap water costs more every year."
5. **Brief E (re-structure).** Take any Gen corpus video's beat map from `beat-maps.md` and re-plan it as a 38-minute Adam-mode essay. Explain every moved beat.
