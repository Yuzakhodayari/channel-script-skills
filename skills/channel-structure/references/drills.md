## Drill 3 — Opener library
Tag each line with its card, then write your own on a new topic.

- “Do you remember that oil shock we warned you about a few months ago? Well, it is here.” → `R01` Hook inside 15 seconds
- “And the question I want to ask is, if North Korea was doomed, why did it survive?” → `R02` Thesis question stated by 0.08
- “Okay, but why should you care?” → `R03` Why-should-you-care beat
- “Well, to find out, as always, we need to answer a few important questions.” → `R04` Agenda roadmap
- “Before we go any further, I just want to tell you why I wanted to make this video.” → `R05` Why-I-made-this beat
- “This scenario is based on highly cited research by top” → `R06` Credibility tag for scenarios
- “both of those YouTube narratives got it wrong” → `R07` Steelman 'other videos get it wrong' position
- “This video is brought to you by Commentaire Coffee. More on them later.” → `R08` Sponsor window 0.10-0.25
- “So, just how crazy is $2 trillion in valuation?” → `R09` Cliffhanger into sponsor
- “what's also dangerous is how today it feels like every news outlet has a side.” → `R10` Topical sponsor bridge
- “And now, back to the story.” → `R11` Re-entry line after sponsor
- “Which brings us to phase two” → `R12` Numbered phases
- “But unfortunately, the story doesn't end there.” → `R13` Act break every 15-20%
- “Anyway, back to the scenario.” → `R14` Chapter backstory jump
- “A quick sidebar to talk about feedback loops. Woo. Math.” → `R15` Sidebar flagged as sidebar
- “Pause here for a second. Do you see how this works?” → `R16` With-me-so-far checkpoint
- “Everything that we talked about up to this point is actually just the first third” → `R17` Stakes escalation 'first third'
- “Okay, Sable is starting to sound a little sci-fi here, so I want to talk about how much of this is justified.” → `R18` Calibration aside
