## Drill 3 — Opener library
Tag each line with its card, then write your own on a new topic.

- “It all started six months ago, with a tweet.” → `T01` In-medias-res future cold open, then rewind
- “On a sweltering June day in front of a velvet curtain at Barclays Bank in north London. The man took a piece of paper out of his pocket” → `T02` Dated cinematic scene open
- “if gambling your savings is that stupid, why is every single industry in America now getting funded by it?” → `T03` Contradiction question hook
- “It seems like gold is having a comeback. But actually, what I'm learning is that it never went away” → `T04` Perception-flip hook
- “You may have missed it, but a new war got started a couple of weeks ago. And if you don't believe me, take it from this guy.” → `T05` 'Take it from this guy' clip hook
- “A woman tells you about her divorce at 2 am.” → `T06` Second-person POV open
- “Okay, am I crazy or is it really starting to smell a lot like robot apocalypse in here?” → `T07` Absurd-literal hook
- “This is the most important signal in America right now, the flatbed truck.” → `T08` Physical-object reveal hook
- “It's 3 am. Ivan hasn't eaten since lunch. He hasn't noticed.” → `T09` POV character with a ticking clock
- “Rajesh sees the profit spike in his dashboard, he doesn't investigate why. Profits up means good.” → `T10` Human-owner reaction beat
- “our friend Chris punch-them-in-the-mouth Lehane” → `T11` Named recurring side character
- “But then I talked to Nate.” → `T12` Narrator as character
- “the man who broke capitalism” → `T13` Villain case study
- “So you driving home from work and you see these sketchy ass storefronts with neon signs like cash now. You're thinking, 'Damn, who the hell falls for this?' Right before you hit a pole” → `T14` Second-person vignette
- “61 hours since Sam Altman was fired.” → `T15` Escalating dated timeline
- “The company kills the siblings that let the users leave” → `T16` Selection-pressure engine
- “It just watched honesty get killed.” → `T17` Moral contrast by numbers
- “In the 2000s, Wells Fargo accidentally ran the same experiment on their employees.” → `T18` Real-world precedent grounding
