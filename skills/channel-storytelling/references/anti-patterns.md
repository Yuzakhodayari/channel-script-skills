# channel-storytelling: anti-patterns

Before = the failure (often AI default). After = an illustrative fix in channel style (not a corpus quote unless marked). Some after lines paraphrase real corpus beats (Oil 03 gas-station footage, Adam 01 Tuesday July 8th, Gen 1 Photoshop piracy, After 02 dentist credit card) to show the pattern.

### X1. Syllabus cold open
- Before: "In this video, we will explore the history of SpaceX and its impact."
- After: "90% of everything going to space this year rides on one company's rocket. And there's not a lot of room up there."

### X2. Background-first open
- Before: "To understand this story, we need to go back to 1945."
- After: "For 16 hours on a Tuesday, an AI lost its mind in public. To understand why, we have to go back a few years."

### X3. Unearned shock adjective
- Before: "This shocking story will blow your mind."
- After: "One guy used AI to copy Photoshop in two weeks. For free."

### X4. Faceless system
- Before: "Consumers are struggling with rising prices."
- After: "Here's a trucker who spent $1,000 filling one tank."

### X5. Character CV dump
- Before: "John Smith was born in 1970, studied at MIT, worked at IBM..."
- After: "This is John. He spent eight years suing the school that expelled him."

### X6. Unpaid open loop
- Before: "There's another factor we'll come back to. (never mentioned again)"
- After: "Remember the big boats I said I'd come back to? This is where they matter."

### X7. Answer immediately, no gap
- Before: "Why did prices rise? Because of demand. Why did demand rise? Because..."
- After: "So why did prices go up? To get there, we need to talk about a guy in Riyadh."

### X8. Reveal by adjective
- Before: "The real reason was surprising."
- After: "Turns out the buyer was the same company selling the chips."

### X9. No dated moments
- Before: "At some point, the company changed its policy."
- After: "On November 17th, at noon, the board fired him over a Google Meet."

### X10. Narrator absent / encyclopedic
- Before: "It has been argued that AI may pose risks."
- After: "As I researched this video, the thing that scared me most wasn't the robots."

### X11. Narrator preaching
- Before: "We must all come together and act now."
- After: "I don't think there's an easy fix. But it starts with knowing who's paying for this."

### X12. Flat tension curve
- Before: "Fact. Fact. Fact. Fact."
- After: "Fact. But here's the catch. Bigger fact. And it gets worse."

### X13. Cartoon villain
- Before: "Evil billionaires want to destroy the world."
- After: "He's not trying to wreck anything. He's trying to win. And that's kind of the problem."

### X14. No irony beat
- Before: "The government said the plan was working while prices tripled."
- After: "The government said the plan was working. Prices tripled. So, working for someone."

### X15. Stays micro
- Before: "(one family story for 5 minutes)"
- After: "And it's not just them. It's happening in every town with a data center."

### X16. Stays macro
- Before: "(system only, no person)"
- After: "Which means your power bill. Literally yours."

### X17. Callback missing
- Before: "(ending introduces new image)"
- After: "Remember the gas station in Texas that ran out? It's open again. At $7 a gallon."

### X18. Moral-summary ending
- Before: "In conclusion, greed is bad and we should be careful."
- After: "So the next time your dentist pitches you a credit card, you'll know why."

### X19. Long outro
- Before: "Thank you so much for watching, please like, subscribe, share, comment, hit the bell..."
- After: "Thanks for watching. See you in the next one."

### X20. Fake urgency
- Before: "You need to act NOW before it's too late!"
- After: "The deadline is 2027. That's two budget cycles away."

### X21. Steelman skipped
- Before: "Critics are simply wrong."
- After: "To be fair, the companies say the water gets recycled. Some of it does."

### X22. Listicle structure
- Before: "Reason 1... Reason 2... Reason 3..."
- After: "There are actually three oil shocks hitting at once. And a fourth if you count the boats."

### X23. Emotion without evidence
- Before: "This is terrifying."
- After: "The model tried to copy itself to another server. That's in the safety report."

### X24. Same beat length everywhere
- Before: "(every section 90 seconds, same shape)"
- After: "Short punch section. Long mechanism. Short reveal."

### X25. Hypothetical drag
- Before: "Imagine a world where... (2 minutes)"
- After: "Imagine your rent doubled overnight. That's basically what happened in Phoenix."
