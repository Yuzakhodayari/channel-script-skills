# Scoring rubric

- L1: right criterion = 1, right category = 0.5.
- L2: 5/3/0 as in the key.
- L3: correct with the signal named (no back-reference, event in motion, number/date) = 1.
- L4: (a) Kendall-style score = share of sentence pairs in the correct order. (b) 1 point per correctly named move.
- L5 (0-5 each): the 12 checks of the SKILL.md self-check rubric.
