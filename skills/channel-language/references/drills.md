## Drill 3 — Opener library
Tag each line with its card, then write your own on a new topic.

- “Sam was expecting a routine call. It is not a routine call.” → `L01` Long build, short punch
- “So, why was Sam Altman fired? And what does that tell us about who gets to decide our future?” → `L02` And/So/But sentence starters
- “menus, clicking buttons, steering. Tapping. Messaging. Sorting. Waiting on hold. Hammering a nail.” → `L03` Fragment lists
- “He's still in control. He's... Pretty sure. He's still in control.” → `L04` Ellipsis hesitation for unease
- “If we are clearly never going to pay down our debt, why are people still lending us money? And well, what happens if they stop?” → `L05` Question then answer in one breath
- “Everything that we talked about up to this point is actually just the first third” → `L06` Self-correcting count
- “you'd really, really, really think so” → `L07` Repetition for rhythm (triple word)
- “Same as training, just a larger Petri dish.” → `L08` Mantra refrain line
- “So, no, we are not going to talk about the lake thing.” → `L09` Contractions everywhere
- “your grandma's retirement” → `L10` Second person on every other line
- “I think people are really missing the stakes of this one.” → `L11` Narrator first person and stance
- “as someone who gets pitched a new gambling deal like every week, by the end, I'll show you the only thing that could fix this.” → `L12` Self-positioning credential line
- “Just build the damn houses.” → `L13` Mild profanity in Gen lean only
