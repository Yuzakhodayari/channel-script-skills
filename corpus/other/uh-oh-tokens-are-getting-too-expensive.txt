(6:34) LLMs like ChatGPT don’t understand language. They can’t read what you say, see an image you send,
(6:40) or hear the audio you speak. “The word darkness, for example,

(7:00) According to NVIDIA: “The goal is to achieve the fastest processing time and lowest cost per

(7:15) And as a disclaimer I’m not an AI expert, not even close.
(7:20) But what I am extremely interested in is the
(7:23) business side, and why many businesses have done a 180 on their token spend.
(7:28) There are a few factors to this. The first is that tokens consumed
(7:32) don’t scale linearly with productive output. A study by Jellyfish found that larger token
(7:37) budgets did result in more pull requests, aka, proposed changes to a shared codebase,

(7:47) “Tokens, in this sense, behave less like a linear input and more like rocket fuel.
(7:52) Going faster is possible, but it requires exponentially more resources to do so.”
(7:58) Another problem, one that’s perhaps most surprising,
(8:01) comes from the more advanced reasoning models. Remember what NVIDIA said about “fastest
(8:06) processing time”? Well, that’s where we run into a problem.
(8:10) Claude Opus, GPT o3, the ones that can tackle harder problems, spend minutes
(8:15) or even hours thinking to solve a problem. “Reasoning AI models, the latest advancement in
(8:20) LLMs, can tackle more complex queries by treating tokens differently than before. Here, in addition

(8:39) So powerful, that the US Government has issued an export control on Claude Fable,

(8:56) and really deeply thought about it for 30 seconds, and then said “2”.
(9:01) You’d think “You already know the answer, why’d you think so hard about it”.
(9:05) That’s basically what’s happening. AI right now has a bit of a reasoning
(9:10) problem. Or, rather, an inefficiency problem. If you ask some, not all, but some of the

(9:20) break down the question, deliberate over it, and may even take 10 or 20 seconds.
(9:25) They can break tasks into smaller subtasks of a larger problem. Greater
(9:29) potential output, but more tokens consumed. What’s weird, is the “lighter” AI models,

(9:54) dollars in excess computation annually”. So even though all those engineers get

(10:33) All of this compounds even more, when we look at Agentic AI.
